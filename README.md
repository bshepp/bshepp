## Brian Sheppard

**Physics, math, and AI - built in the open.**

I build tools and do research at the intersection of AI, scientific computing, and art. Most of it
starts as a question I can't stop poking at and turns into code, a paper, or both.

### What I'm working on

- **[3body-poisson-algebra](https://github.com/bshepp/3body-poisson-algebra)** - the dimension of the
  pairwise Poisson algebra of the gravitational *N*-body problem: a growing sequence (3, 6, 17, 116…)
  whose structure we're still mapping. Live dashboard → **[nbody.briansheppard.com](https://nbody.briansheppard.com)**.
- **[alcubierre](https://github.com/bshepp/alcubierre)** - a boundary-mode reformulation of the warp-drive
  metric, treating the negative energy as a boundary (Casimir-like) effect rather than exotic matter.
  Phase-1 linearization verified symbolically and numerically.
