## Brian Sheppard

**Physics, math, and AI - built in the open.**

Navy RF systems → EPA AI policy → AI/ML since 2019. I build tools and do research at the intersection
of AI, scientific computing, and art. Most of it starts as a question I can't stop poking at and turns
into code, a paper, or both.

### What I'm working on

- **[graph-graph](https://github.com/bshepp/graph-graph)** - do simple local graph rules at large scale
  produce interesting emergent behavior? Sparse simulation out to millions of nodes, dimension analysis,
  and experiment waves that are pre-registered before they run.
- **[terravector](https://github.com/bshepp/terravector)** - terrain patch similarity search: decomposition-based
  embeddings of elevation data, indexed with HNSW. Grows out of
  **[RESIDUALS](https://github.com/bshepp/RESIDUALS)**, my differential feature-detection toolkit for
  archaeological prospection.
- **[alcubierre](https://github.com/bshepp/alcubierre)** - reframing the warp-drive metric's exotic-matter
  requirement as a boundary (Casimir-like) effect. Phase-1 linearization verified symbolically and
  numerically; v0.3.0 archived on Zenodo.

### Recently

- **[ESTA](https://github.com/bshepp/ESTA)** - a local LLM wrapper that exposes auditable internal state
  (token confidence, refusal-direction projection) alongside OpenAI-compatible responses.
- **[integer-sequence-visualizer](https://github.com/bshepp/integer-sequence-visualizer)** - live OEIS
  visualizer with a null-model layer: is the structure in the sequence, or in the rendering?
- **[rna-joint-normality](https://github.com/bshepp/rna-joint-normality)** - proof of a Bu–Kauers–Zeilberger
  conjecture: hairpins and basepairs in random RNA secondary structures are asymptotically jointly normal.
- **[3body-poisson-algebra](https://github.com/bshepp/3body-poisson-algebra)** - the dimension of the pairwise
  Poisson algebra of the *N*-body problem (3, 6, 17, 116…). Dashboard →
  **[nbody.briansheppard.com](https://nbody.briansheppard.com)**.
